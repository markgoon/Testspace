Wahiba Sands, Endless dunes, ancient stillness

Wahiba Sands, also called Sharqiya Sands, is a large and famous desert in Oman. It covers an area of 12,500 square kilometers with towering red and golden sand dunes that reach over 100 meters high.

Jebel Shams , The Grand Canyon of Arabia

The highest mountain in Oman. Often called the "Mountain of the Sun," it features the dramatic Wadi Ghul gorge, widely known as the Grand Canyon of Arabia.

Nizwa · Living history, still standing
